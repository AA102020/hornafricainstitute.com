Regional mission

Our mission

The Horn of Africa Institute is an independent research and policy organization based in Washington, DC. Its mission is to produce rigorous analysis, inform public decisions, and advance policies that support peace, capable institutions, shared prosperity, and resilience across the Horn of Africa.

The Institute approaches the region as an interconnected political, economic, environmental, and security system. National institutions remain central to policy, while conflict, trade, displacement, climate shocks, water systems, technology, and external engagement routinely cross borders. Our research reflects both levels of analysis.

Our contribution

The Institute brings regional knowledge into sustained conversation with policy research, technical analysis, and strategic foresight. We examine how public problems develop, how institutions respond, which interventions remain feasible, and what consequences different choices may produce.

Our purpose is to improve the quality of policy reasoning. We identify assumptions, assess evidence, compare alternatives, and communicate uncertainty clearly. This approach supports decisionmakers while preserving the independence required for credible analysis.

Institutional principles

Independence

Research questions, findings, and recommendations follow evidence and analytical judgment.

Regional grounding

Analysis reflects the region’s histories, institutions, political economies, and lived realities.

Analytical rigor

Methods must fit the question, expose assumptions, and withstand informed scrutiny.

Policy relevance

Research should clarify choices, tradeoffs, implementation requirements, and foreseeable consequences.

Engagement

The Institute works across research, policy dialogue, strategic analysis, technical assistance, training, and public engagement. Its audiences include public agencies, regional and international organizations, research institutions, civil society, businesses, technical specialists, and diaspora communities.
