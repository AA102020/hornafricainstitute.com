Regional focus

Purpose

The Horn of Africa Institute is an independent research, policy, and public-engagement organization based in the United States. Its purpose is to deepen understanding of the forces shaping the Horn of Africa and to support policies that advance peace, capable institutions, sustainable development, and resilience.

The region’s challenges cross national borders and administrative categories. Conflict affects markets and public institutions. Climate shocks alter migration, livelihoods, and security. Technology changes state capacity, information environments, and economic opportunity. The Institute studies these relationships directly.

Mission

Our mission is to produce rigorous, decision-relevant analysis and to create a platform where regional knowledge, technical expertise, and policy judgment can inform practical choices.

Operating principles

Independence

Analysis should follow evidence and disclose uncertainty without serving partisan or factional interests.

Regional grounding

Policy options must reflect institutions, histories, political economies, and lived realities within the region.

Methodological range

Qualitative inquiry, quantitative analysis, field knowledge, and computational tools answer different parts of complex questions.

Practical relevance

Research becomes valuable when it clarifies decisions, consequences, implementation requirements, and tradeoffs.

Who we engage

The Institute engages policymakers, public agencies, regional organizations, research institutions, civil society, businesses, technical specialists, and diaspora communities. Engagement may take the form of research partnerships, policy dialogue, analytical support, training, or jointly designed initiatives.
