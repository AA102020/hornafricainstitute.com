About the Institute

Regional knowledge. Independent research. Practical policy insight.

The Horn of Africa Institute is an independent research and policy organization based in Washington, DC.

Headquarters

Washington, DC
Affiliates across the Horn of Africa

Our mission

The Institute produces rigorous analysis, informs public decisions, and develops policy options that support peace, capable institutions, economic development, and resilience across the Horn of Africa.

The Institute approaches the region as an interconnected political, economic, environmental, security, and technological system. National institutions remain central to policy, while conflict, trade, climate shocks, water systems, infrastructure, technology, and external engagement routinely cross borders.

Regional scope

The Institute’s principal area of study includes Djibouti, Eritrea, Ethiopia, Kenya, Somalia, South Sudan, Sudan, and Uganda. Its research also examines the regional systems connecting these countries with the Red Sea, Gulf of Aden, Nile Basin, Indian Ocean, East African interior, and Arabian Peninsula.

Our contribution

The Institute combines regional knowledge with policy research, quantitative analysis, and strategic foresight. We examine how public problems develop, how institutions respond, which policy options are feasible, and what consequences different choices may produce.

Our purpose is to improve the quality of policy analysis and decision-making. We identify assumptions, assess evidence, compare alternatives, and communicate uncertainty clearly.

Institutional principles

Independence

Research questions, findings, and recommendations follow evidence and analytical judgment.

Regional grounding

Analysis reflects the region’s histories, institutions, political economies, security systems, and relationships with external actors.

Analytical rigor

Methods must fit the question, expose assumptions, distinguish evidence from inference, and withstand informed scrutiny.

Policy relevance

Research should clarify choices, tradeoffs, implementation requirements, institutional constraints, and foreseeable consequences.

Intellectual pluralism

The Institute evaluates competing explanations and policy alternatives through evidence and reasoned analysis.

Responsible analysis

Research involving sensitive political, security, institutional, or personal information follows clear standards for evidence, confidentiality, data protection, and publication.

Independence and accountability

The Institute protects the integrity of its research through transparent attribution, source verification, conflict-of-interest disclosure, appropriate review, and correction of substantive errors. The Institute retains responsibility for its research methods, findings, interpretations, and conclusions.

Engagement

The Institute works across research, policy dialogue, strategic analysis, technical assistance, training, and public engagement. Its audiences include public agencies, regional and international organizations, research institutions, businesses, journalists, and technical specialists.
