About the program

The Horn of Africa combines expanding markets, a young population, strategic trade routes, natural resources, rapid urbanization, and significant technological change. These opportunities coexist with institutional constraints, fiscal pressure, uneven infrastructure, limited financial access, political uncertainty, and exposure to external shocks.

The program examines how governments, markets, and public institutions can use regional resources, trade routes, investment, and technology to support broad-based development. Research connects economic policy with administrative capability, political incentives, implementation systems, and distributional consequences.

Research areas

Institutions and state capability

Public administration, regulatory quality, service delivery, accountability, implementation capacity, and institutional reform.

Macroeconomic and fiscal policy

Public finance, debt sustainability, revenue systems, expenditure priorities, monetary conditions, and economic resilience.

Finance and investment

Banking systems, consumer and enterprise finance, financial inclusion, investment climate, development finance, and capital formation.

Trade and infrastructure

Regional integration, ports and corridors, logistics, energy, digital infrastructure, supply chains, and connectivity that supports trade, industry, and public services.

Inclusive growth

Employment, enterprise development, urbanization, human capital, social protection, and equitable access to opportunity.

Digital transformation

Digital public infrastructure, government data systems, technology policy, administrative modernization, innovation governance, and public accountability.

Research and policy analysis

The program produces institutional diagnostics, economic and fiscal assessments, public-investment analysis, reform options, implementation strategies, and scenario analysis for policy decisions. Foresight Lab contributes data engineering, modeling, forecasting, simulation, and geospatial analysis.

Key questions

Strengthening institutions for sustainable economic development

  • Which institutions enable effective implementation?
  • How can public finance support resilience and growth?
  • What expands responsible access to finance?
  • Which infrastructure investments produce regional benefits?
  • How can digital systems strengthen public capability?
  • How should reforms distribute risks and opportunities?
