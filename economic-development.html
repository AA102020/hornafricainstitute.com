Program 02

Economic Development, Governance, and Institutions

Research and advisory work on capable institutions, inclusive growth, financial systems, regional integration, and technology-enabled public administration.

ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT

Institutional capacity is central to sustainable economic transformation.

Program perspective

Development strategies succeed through institutions that can set priorities, mobilize resources, regulate fairly, deliver services, and sustain implementation. Our work connects economic analysis with the governance arrangements through which policy becomes operational.

We combine regional insight with institutional diagnostics, policy analysis, modeling, and scenario design to help public and private actors assess constraints, compare interventions, and manage implementation risk.

Focus areas

Governance and state capacity

Regulatory quality, public financial management, accountability, administrative capability, and service delivery.

Economic diversification

Infrastructure, productive sectors, investment priorities, industrial development, and public-private cooperation.

Regional integration

Cross-border trade, transport corridors, policy harmonization, shared infrastructure, and economic connectivity.

Banking and finance

Financial stability, banking regulation, monetary frameworks, financial inclusion, capital formation, and international integration.

Institutional resilience

Continuity during political and economic transition, reform sequencing, stakeholder participation, and implementation capacity.

Digital transformation

Digital public infrastructure, e-government, data systems, revenue administration, transparency, and citizen access.

Advisory capabilities

Our advisory engagements include institutional diagnostics, development strategy, public investment assessment, debt and fiscal analysis, banking-policy support, program design, policy simulation, implementation planning, and capacity development.
