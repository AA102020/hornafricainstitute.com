Institutional engagement

Public institutions

Policy assessments, strategic briefings, institutional diagnostics, implementation analysis, and regional political and economic assessments.

International organizations

Independent research, program evaluation, early-warning frameworks, capability studies, data analysis, and strategic planning.

Gulf institutions and development funds

Country and sector assessments, infrastructure risk, investment governance, port and corridor analysis, and development-finance research.

Research institutions

Joint research, comparative projects, conferences, seminars, and publication partnerships.

Businesses and investors

Political-economy analysis, institutional assessments, infrastructure studies, scenarios, and strategic-risk briefings.

Policy dialogue and training

Expert roundtables, institutional briefings, scenario exercises, executive workshops, and technical training.

Partnership criteria

The Institute evaluates proposed engagements according to their policy relevance, alignment with its mission, independence requirements, available expertise, and expected public benefit. Research findings and conclusions remain subject to the Institute’s standards for evidence, attribution, review, and disclosure.
