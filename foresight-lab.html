FORESIGHT LAB

The Institute’s center for data analysis, modeling, forecasting, and strategic foresight.

Mandate

Foresight Lab is a permanent analytical department within the Horn of Africa Institute. It supports the Institute’s three research programs and produces independent research on emerging risks, structural change, and decision-making under uncertainty.

The Lab combines regional knowledge with quantitative, computational, and scenario-based methods. Its role is to identify emerging trends and warning indicators, test assumptions, compare plausible futures, and clarify the conditions under which policies may succeed or fail.

Core capabilities

Strategic foresight

Horizon scanning, trend analysis, early indicators, scenario development, and assessment of emerging policy challenges.

Data science and machine learning

Data integration, statistical modeling, predictive analysis, pattern detection, model evaluation, validation, and responsible use.

Geospatial intelligence

GIS, remote sensing, spatial analysis, risk mapping, mobility patterns, infrastructure, and environmental monitoring.

Systems modeling

Agent-based models, system dynamics, network analysis, simulation, causal analysis, and sensitivity testing.

Risk and warning systems

Indicator design, event monitoring, structured risk assessment, escalation analysis, and systems that connect early warning with institutional response.

Decision support

Policy simulations, scenario comparison, tradeoff analysis, implementation assessment, and clear communication of uncertainty.

Standards for analytical use

Models and forecasts inform judgment; they do not replace it. The Lab documents assumptions, identifies data limitations, evaluates uncertainty, and tests model behavior before drawing policy conclusions. Analytical outputs are interpreted alongside institutional knowledge, field evidence, and regional expertise.

Institute-wide role

Within Conflict and Stability, the Lab supports early warning, geospatial risk analysis, and simulation of conflict dynamics. Within Economic Development, Governance, and Institutions, it supports forecasting, policy simulation, infrastructure analysis, and institutional diagnostics. Within Climate Resilience, Environment, and Water, it supports remote sensing, hydrological modeling, vulnerability assessment, and compound-risk scenarios.

Analytical products

Analysis designed to support institutional decisions.

  • Strategic outlooks and risk monitors
  • Geospatial assessments and infrastructure analysis
  • Economic, fiscal, climate, and security scenarios
  • Institutional capability assessments
  • Executive decision briefs
  • Scenario planning and policy exercises
