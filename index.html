About the Institute

Regional knowledge. Strategic perspective. Practical relevance.

The Horn of Africa Institute is an independent research, policy, and public-engagement organization focused on the political, economic, security, technological, and social transformations shaping the region. The Institute connects careful analysis with the decisions facing governments, institutions, businesses, researchers, and communities.
